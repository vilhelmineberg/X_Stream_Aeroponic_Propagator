Privacy Policy

Plug Tracker – Propagation (the app) — last updated 24 September 2026.

Summary

This app does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data. Period.

Data storage

Everything you enter in the app — propagation boxes, plant names, and planting dates — is stored only in a local database on your own device. The app has no network access, no accounts, no sign-in, and no connection to any server.

Data collection and third parties

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. It requests no Android permissions.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's app settings, permanently deletes all data. If you use Android's device backup, your data may be included in your own device backup handled by Android — the developer never has access to it.
