## Short description (max 80 characters)

```
Track which plant grows in every plug of your aeroponic propagator.
```

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Never lose track of what you planted where. Plug Tracker gives you a visual map of your propagation box, so every cutting and seedling has a name and a date.

Made for aeroponic propagators such as the X-Stream Aeroponic Propagator, with ready-made layouts for the standard sizes:

• 20 plugs (4 × 5)
• 40 plugs (5 × 8)
• 80 plugs (8 × 10)
• 120 plugs (8 × 15)

SEE YOUR WHOLE BOX AT A GLANCE
The app shows your propagator as a grid of round plugs, exactly like the real thing. Empty plugs are blank, planted plugs are filled and color-coded by plant type — so you can tell your basil from your chili from across the room.

PLANT MANY PLUGS IN ONE GO
Tap the empty plugs you just filled, type the plant name once, and all of them are registered together. Previously used plant names are suggested as you type, so the same plant is always spelled the same way.

KNOW HOW OLD EVERY PLANT IS
Each plug remembers its planting date. A list below the grid shows every plant with its position, date, and how many days have passed — perfect for judging when cuttings are ready to root check or transplant.

MULTIPLE BOXES
Run more than one propagator? Create as many boxes as you need, give each a name, and switch between them instantly.

SIMPLE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no ads
• Works completely offline
• No permissions required
• All data stays in a local database on your device

Whether you are cloning cuttings, starting seeds, or running a full propagation schedule, Plug Tracker replaces the sticky notes and guesswork with one clear picture of your propagator.

X-Stream is a trademark of its respective owner. This app is an independent companion tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the manufacturer.
```
